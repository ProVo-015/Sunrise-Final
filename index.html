About Us

Since 2000

Established In 2000, Based In Mokopane, Limpopo

It is indeed our pleasure to introduce you to our company. Sunrise Security Services was established in 2000 with deep experience in the security industry. Our company has grown over the years to become the preferred security service provider to a wide range of clients. This growth is attributed to the vision, culture and strong leadership of our management team.

Our staff's commitment, passion, loyalty and dedication has carried our brand from humble beginnings to a respected player in the security industry. We keep abreast of the latest trends in the security industry, while adhering to relevant legislation, regulations and codes of practice and conduct.

Our head office is based in Mokopane, Limpopo, with a branch office in Hammanskraal. Our operations span throughout South Africa.

How We Operate

Operations & Standards

1

Monitoring Systems

Effective monitoring at our command centre runs around the clock. Officers patrol on a set schedule, and panic buttons summon an armed response unit the moment help is needed.

2

Vehicle Fleet

Our fleet is serviced regularly and satellite-tracked via GPS linked to the control centre, so controllers always dispatch the best-positioned, best-suited unit.

3

Health & Safety

A health and safety policy is enforced as a minimum standard in every region where we operate.

4

Training & Development

Officers are multi-skilled and selected on suitability, integrity and career commitment, with resources provided for further education through institutions of higher learning.

5

Quality Management

We run customer service assessments, quality audits and performance appraisals, and act on what they find.

6

Corporate Social Investment

Through the Sunrise investment programme we support community-uplifting projects addressing social and spiritual challenges in the communities we serve.
